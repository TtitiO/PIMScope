# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be constructive, assume good intent, give technically
specific feedback, respect differing viewpoints, and acknowledge sources and
prior work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Report unacceptable behavior
privately to the project maintainers through the repository owner's public
contact channel. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
